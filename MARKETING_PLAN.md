# CodeSecure Solutions — Marketing Plan for 1 Marketing Executive

**Company:** Codesecure Solutions (www.codesecure.in) — Chennai, India
**What we sell:** VAPT (Web ₹20k / Mobile ₹25k / Network ₹30k), API/cloud/source-code audits, GenAI/LLM security audits, compliance audits (ISO 27001, PCI DSS, HIPAA, SOC 2, DPDP).
**Our advantages to repeat in every message:** ISO 27001:2022 certified, 150+ clients, fixed transparent pricing, OSCP/CEH/CISSP certified testers, free retest within 90 days, AI-augmented testing, report in days not weeks.

**Who we target:** CTOs, engineering heads, and founders of SaaS startups, fintech, healthcare apps, e-commerce; compliance managers in banks/NBFCs; IT heads in manufacturing/logistics. Start with Chennai + Bangalore + Hyderabad, then all India, then Gulf/SEA.

---

## Daily tasks (Mon–Fri, ~2 hours total)

| Task | Time | Details |
|------|------|---------|
| LinkedIn outreach | 45 min | Send 20 connection requests/day to CTOs, founders, compliance heads. No pitch in the request. After they accept, send a short value message (see templates below). |
| Cold email | 30 min | Send 25 personalized emails/day to companies from the prospect list. Follow up on day 3, day 7, day 14. |
| Engage on LinkedIn | 20 min | Comment usefully on 5 posts by target customers and cybersecurity influencers (this builds visibility for free). |
| Update CRM/tracker | 15 min | Log every contact, reply, and meeting in a Google Sheet or free CRM (HubSpot Free / Zoho CRM free tier). |
| Reply to inbound | as needed | Answer website/WhatsApp/email enquiries within 1 hour (we promise this on the site — keep it true). |

## Weekly tasks

| Task | Frequency | Details |
|------|-----------|---------|
| LinkedIn company posts | 3 per week | Mon: educational (e.g. "Top 5 API vulnerabilities we found this month"). Wed: proof (client result, anonymized finding, certificate, review). Fri: offer (free consultation / sample report / pricing post — fixed pricing is rare in this industry, show it off). |
| 1 blog article on codesecure.in | 1 per week | 800–1200 words targeting search keywords (list below). This is the #1 long-term lead source. |
| 1 short video or carousel | 1 per week | 60-second explainer: "What is VAPT?", "What happens in a pentest?", "DPDP Act — what startups must do". Post to LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube Shorts. |
| Build prospect list | 1 session | Add 100 new companies/contacts to the list using LinkedIn Sales Navigator, startup directories (YourStory, Inc42 funding news — freshly funded startups need security audits for enterprise deals), AngelList/Wellfound, Clutch. |
| Follow up warm leads | 1 session | Call/WhatsApp everyone who replied but didn't book. Offer a free 30-min consultation. |

## Monthly tasks

| Task | Details |
|------|---------|
| 1 case study | Write up one completed project (anonymized): problem → what we found → how fast we fixed → outcome. Turn it into a PDF + LinkedIn post + blog post. |
| 1 lead magnet | Free downloadable: "Web App Security Checklist", "SOC 2 Readiness Checklist", "Sample VAPT Report". Collect email addresses to download. |
| 1 webinar or LinkedIn Live | 30-min session: "How hackers break into web apps — live demo" or "ISO 27001 in 90 days". Partner with a startup community for audience. |
| Directory & review maintenance | Keep profiles updated + collect 2 new client reviews on: Google Business Profile, Clutch.co, GoodFirms, DesignRush, G2. Reviews are the biggest trust factor for security buyers. |
| Email newsletter | 1 email to the full contact list: recent vulnerabilities in the news + 1 tip + 1 offer. |
| Report to management | Numbers review (see KPIs below), what worked, what to change next month. |

## Quarterly tasks

- Attend/sponsor 1 local event: OWASP Chennai chapter meetups, BSides, Nasscom/startup community events. Collect cards, follow up within 48 hours.
- Partnership outreach: web development agencies, CA firms doing compliance, cloud consultants, DevOps agencies — offer them 10–15% referral commission. One good partner = steady lead flow.
- Refresh website: update client count, add new case studies, test the contact form.
- Run a promotional campaign: e.g. "End of quarter: free retest extended to 120 days" or bundle pricing (Web + API VAPT).

## Free/cheap tools

- **CRM:** HubSpot Free or Zoho CRM (free tier)
- **Design:** Canva (posts, carousels, PDFs)
- **Email:** Zoho Campaigns / Brevo free tier for newsletters; plain Gmail/Zoho Mail for cold outreach (warm up the domain, max 25–30/day)
- **Scheduling:** Calendly free — put the booking link everywhere
- **SEO:** Google Search Console + Google Keyword Planner (free)
- **Prospecting:** LinkedIn (free to start; Sales Navigator ~₹6,500/month once results show)

## First 30 days for the new employee

- **Week 1:** Learn the services (shadow a delivery call, read 2 sample reports). Set up CRM, tracker, Calendly, Google Business Profile. Build first 300-contact prospect list.
- **Week 2:** Start daily outreach rhythm. Publish first blog post. First 3 LinkedIn posts.
- **Week 3:** First follow-up cycle. Set up Clutch/GoodFirms profiles. Ask 3 existing happy clients for Google reviews.
- **Week 4:** First case study + first lead magnet published. Review numbers, adjust messaging based on what got replies.
